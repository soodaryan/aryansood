Research threads

My research started with physics-informed neural networks. I later explored KAN-style symbolic modeling, which led into PIKA-GAN. I am most interested in models that expose structure instead of hiding everything inside a black box.

What I have been exploring

  1. PIKA-GAN - Physics-Inspired Kolmogorov-Arnold GANs

    Under review - NeurIPS workshop verifier track

    Built a physics-informed GAN around KANs for inverse kinematics and simulation. Project materials report 23% lower RMSE and 20% higher stability than PINN baselines.

  2. CorruptLoRA - Corruption-Guided LoRA for Masked Diffusion LMs

    Under review - NeurIPS / masked diffusion language models

    Uses the corruption mask in masked diffusion language models to choose where LoRA residuals are active. The draft reports lower validation loss than random and uniform token selection under the same active-token budget.

  3. SAR Denoising and Colorization

    Pix2Pix GAN / PiggyBack GAN style pipelines

    Worked on SAR image denoising, colorization, and segmentation. Project materials report PSNR 27.68 and 85.64% targeted segmentation accuracy for the SARVA workflow.

  4. PINNs and PIKAN-style Exploration

    Physics-informed neural networks and KANs

    Worked through PINNs and KAN-based symbolic modeling before the current papers. That thread shaped how I think about scientific ML.
